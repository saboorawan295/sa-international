Trade Protocol

The SA Commodities Sourcing Workflow

Transparent, contractually protected cross-border commodity procurement.

01

Specification & FCO

Issue of Formal Corporate Offer (FCO), laboratory spec sheets, and preliminary sample dispatch.

02

Contract & LC Opening

Bilateral sales contract execution followed by buyer's irrevocable, confirmed LC issuance.

03

Procure & Lab Audit

Milling, grading, and independent third-party SGS inspection during stuffing at origin quayside.

04

Vessel Bill of Lading

Vessel sailing, presentation of original shipping documents via banking channels, and customs clearance.
